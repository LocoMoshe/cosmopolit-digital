WHY MOSQUES

Built for a mosque, not adapted to one.

Agencies build websites for companies and then try to fit them to a mosque. We started from the other end: how a committee actually works. Who posts a funeral notice at midnight, how the khutbah is sent out, where membership fees and donors are kept.

That is why the admin is made so anyone who can send a WhatsApp message can use it. No days of training and no fear of breaking something.

Questions

Our committee isn’t very good with computers. Will they manage?

The admin is made for exactly that: big buttons, clear names and a preview before anything is published. You also get training, a written guide, and you can send questions on WhatsApp.

What happens to our domain and email addresses?

The domain is transferred to you, so the mosque owns it. Addresses like info@ can be forwarded to your existing Gmail for free.

Can we keep the look we have now?

Yes. If you like your current design, we keep it and simply refresh it. That is what Džemat Izet Nanić chose.

Can we see the admin before deciding?

Of course. We send you a short video of the admin, or show it live on a call.
