FAQ

We have an old site. Can you keep the text?

Yes. I move the existing text over word for word, and you decide what changes.

We have no time to write copy.

You don’t need to. I draft it after our conversation; you just read and approve.

Do we need a booking form?

Not necessarily. Most patients prefer to call or send a message, so there is a call button on every page.

Do we have to publish prices?

No. Prices can be on the site, approximate, or left out, as you prefer.

Can we post photos of patients?

Only with the patient’s written consent. For video we usually film the space, equipment and team, without patients.
